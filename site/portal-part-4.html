Portal Part 4

July 15, 2026 · Karen Pearson

Asking…for anything…is completely beyond the scope of every atom within my human form. Solicitation simply has no berth in my realm. My cells have two options – figure IT out or die.

Always willing – I took to figuring out – leaving “IT” out of the sentence provides space for the inevitable constancy of life. And life obliged with a cacophony of constancy.

But, while creating the Portal, I made room for asking. Certainly, this was uncomfortable, but what necessary change isn’t?

I needed two sixteen foot 2x4s. Off I went to ask Kind Kim. He lent. I borrowed and loaded. Task accomplished.

The next day “asking” residuals must have still been roaming the ethers because a neighbor stopped by while I was jacking up the Portal. An engineer for the entirety of existence, the neighbor was taking in the Portal scene while giving me a lowdown on general life conditions at home. Later that evening, a phone call from the neighbor promised some options that would probably prove helpful.

The option – a hand crank winch. Yes, definitely residual “asking energy” was in the ethers…

Live deliberately.
