FACES

July 17, 2026 · Karen Pearson

Faces. Those masks we wear and ask others to donn. The kitchen and bedroom storage drawers are no exception. They needed a face.

Drawer dummies are the final act of two full days of construction. In and out. Measure. Cut. Fit. Hone. Measure. Fit. Sand. Measure. Make a new one. Measure. Cut. Fit. And on it goes…the process of applying a face to drawers.

The completion of these faces whittles the finish work for the house down to a cabinet, two corner shelves, joists fascades, and a possible counter top redo.

The storage drawers beam with newness. Symmetrical lines. Simple. Elegant. Faces.

Faces. Where did mine go? The young one. It’s been replaced with a version better equipped and more endowed to handle the weight of responsibility that comes with artistic dignity only age can bring.

Autumn more than half gone, like my life and my face. Someday soon – my entirety – whittled down without a trace.

For now though, like my cabinets and drawers, I donn the fascade of aesthetically pleasing – at least to me – in an attempt to save face.

Live deliberately.
Karen
