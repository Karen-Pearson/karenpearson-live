What to Expect

Six frequently asked questions.

Who is Karen Pearson?

A philosopher, deep thinker, writer, author, and artist on Decatur Island in the Salish Sea. I identify the structures people operate from but rarely see. Before the island, I worked in various service industries while obtaining my BA and MA as well as taught English and Literature at the College of Southern Nevada while pursuing predoctoral studies, through the 2000s.

What does Karen Pearson actually do?

I make the underlying linguistic and cognitive structures that organize a person’s behavior, decisions, and outcomes visible, so authorship becomes possible. Each piece is exploratory, isolating one pattern and tracing the result back to the language structure producing it.

What I don’t do

The work is not advice and not motivation. I do not prescribe behavior. I do not enforce change. I do not hold court. You do not answer to me. I identify structure. I do not manage the occupants.

What does responsibility mean in Karen Pearson's work?

Responsibility recognizes the source of all as the self, the use of blame as ignorance, and the choice to respond as life’s only purpose.

Who is working with Karen Pearson for?

People willing to examine the language they live inside. When you are ready to front the architecture you are already using, come in.

How do you start working with Karen Pearson?

Through the door marked Work with Karen. Five questions, one at a time. Everything you write there is confidential between you and me. Every submission receives a reply. I appreciate your interest in your life.

Where can I hear Karen Pearson before reaching out?

The vlog carries the videos. The newsletter carries the free guide, Why Responsibility Is The Most Lucrative Asset in Your Life. Watch, read, comment, and write when the writing calls.

Live deliberately. Karen
