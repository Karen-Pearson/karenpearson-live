Portal Part 3

July 14, 2026 · Karen Pearson

Piscean delusions littered with camera-ready shots of each ratcheted strap, nails hammered, wall jack retrofitted and inserted, six foot beam shim carried, positioned, and propped, and each leveling step lay between yellowing grass blades and inches thick slop – meticulously idealized but never recorded for a capsule no one knows and posterity no one wants.

In reality, mud takes center stage – dictating priorities – such is the life of a portal resurrection.

I imagine, dream about getting the camera at several times during several stages – only to defer to the mud.

Energy, ever present, ever changing, ever.

Torii gates represent the ever – the infinite source of creativity – the origins of all.

Raising the gate mandates weight distribution to the topside of the downloped hill, alleviating pressure from the two-tiered top, and keeping the gate top semi-level with the legs pushing against their three foot holes. This is a stop and readjust at every inch endeavor.

Slow. Steady. The portal reached 45 degree lift.
Enough. Enough height, enough mud, enough rain. Enough for today.

Live deliberately.
